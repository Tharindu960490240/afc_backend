Copy these files over your backend folder (do not delete anything).
Then run:  npm run migrate     (adds migrations 011 to 025; 023 to 025 are Phase 5 student applications)
No new environment variables. INBOUND_EMAIL_SECRET is still optional (inbound email logging only).
After deploying, sign in as the administrator and open "Application settings" to review the wording. Letters of Offer cannot be issued until the wording is confirmed there.
Optional demo data: npm run seed:demo
